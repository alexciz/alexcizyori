01 — Strategy & Mechatronics

Continuous Ring Flow & Mobile Goal Management

High Stakes revolves around clamping mobile goals, rapid floor ring acquisition via continuous conveyor, stacking on mobile goals, and scoring neutral wall stakes with an articulated arm.

Built on a 6-motor 450 RPM drivetrain on 2.75" omni wheels, featuring a pneumatic mobile goal clamp, a chain-driven ring conveyor intake with an optical sensor for automatic color sorting, and a geared "Lady Brown" arm for neutral wall stake scoring.

CORE SUBSYSTEMS

  • Drivetrain: 6-motor 450 RPM direct drive on 2.75" omni wheels.
  • Pneumatic Mogo Clamp: Dual-acting pneumatic cylinder actuated clamping latch for mobile goals.
  • Ring Conveyor Intake: Front intake roller leading into continuous chain conveyor to stack rings on goals.
  • Lady Brown Scorer: High-strength geared articulated arm specifically tuned for neutral wall stakes.
  • Optical Color Sorter: V5 optical sensor detecting ring hue to automatically redirect or reject opposing rings.
02 — Iterative Engineering & Rebuilds

Design Evolution: V1 Baseline vs V2 Subsystem Refinements Rebuild

The overarching robot chassis architecture remained fundamentally robust across the season; the major rebuild focused on systematic subsystem redesigns: compliant 3D-printed intake interfaces, active optical sorting gates, and chamfered clamp kinematics.

VERSION 1.0 Early-Season Baseline Architecture
Initial Concept Setup · Core Subsystems Testbed

Initial build proved the multi-subsystem integration: rear mogo clamp, continuous conveyor belt intake, and articulated wall stake arm.

FIGURE 2.1 — V1 ARCHITECTURE: MOGO CLAMP, CONVEYOR & WALL STAKE ARM SCHEMATIC V1 PROTOTYPE
⚠ Identified Failure Modes & Bottlenecks
  • Rigid Intake Bounce: Plastic ring elements bounced off rigid intake plates during high-speed drive approaches, dropping intake rate to ~78%.
  • Manual Color Sorting: Ingesting opponent rings into the conveyor required manual driver reversal, costing precious match seconds.
  • Alignment Sensitivity: Standard rear clamp jaws required strict perpendicular alignment to latch onto mobile goals cleanly.
⚙ V1 Technical Specifications
  • Intake Geometry: Rigid aluminum channel backing.
  • Color Filtering: Manual driver reaction.
  • Clamp Lead-ins: Unchamfered aluminum plates.
  • Wall Stake Scorer: Single-pivot articulated arm.
⚡
THE REBUILD TRIGGER — CHAMPIONSHIP SUBSYSTEM OVERHAUL
Preserved the validated core chassis geometry while executing a comprehensive subsystem overhaul to achieve zero-jam intake compliance, automated optical color ejection, and omni-directional goal capture.
VERSION 2.0 Championship Subsystem Refinements Rebuild
Current Generation System · High-Efficiency Subsystems

Refined the competition build with improved conveyor geometry, reliable chain tensioning, optical color sensor sorting integration, and reinforced pneumatic clamp guides.
